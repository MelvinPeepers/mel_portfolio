I'm a Senior Support Engineer at Netlify, where I help customers troubleshoot, optimize, and scale modern web projects using frameworks like Next.js and Gatsby alongside the Netlify platform. My work focuses on guiding teams through complex build and deployment workflows, diagnosing performance and integration issues, and supporting custom configurations in real-world production environments. I also collaborate closely with developers to test new features and create clear, practical documentation that helps users get the most out of Netlify's tools and services.

Before joining Netlify, I worked at Callcentric, a Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Voice over IP (VoIP) provider. There, I supported customers worldwide by troubleshooting Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) issues, assisting with onboarding, and handling billing and product inquiries. I regularly helped individuals and businesses configure and maintain VoIP devices and network setups, collaborated with developers to test new SIP applications and user agents, and authored detailed technical guides for both customers and internal teams.

I'm passionate about solving customer problems and improving user experiences. After learning software development, I discovered new ways to support users by contributing directly to products, building features, fixing bugs, and streamlining workflows. My technical toolkit includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express.js, and Python, which I use to build and maintain web applications both independently and in collaboration with others.
